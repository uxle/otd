## One-paragraph portrait
Lawrencium is a actinide with atomic number 103: 103 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 103 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,8,3. Its standard atomic mass is
266.0 u, so one mole of Lr atoms weighs 266.0 g. The name comes from
Ernest Lawrence, cyclotron inventor. Lawrencium closes the actinide row — element 103, named for the cyclotron's inventor.
