# Storage and handling of Bromine

- form matters: sealed liquid vial
- environment: dry, cool, ventilated
- no radiological containment needed
- compatibility: keep no reaction as metals do — they are already oxidisers in mind around acids; dissolves and reacts — chlorine water is a disinfectant near water

Sample 45 in this folder (the vial) shows the classic storage look: a glass tube
with the element inside, labelled with its symbol. Museums store reactive
elements exactly like this — argon-sealed ampoules on wooden shelves.
