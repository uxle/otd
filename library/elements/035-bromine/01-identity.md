## One-paragraph portrait
Bromine is a halogen with atomic number 35: 35 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 35 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,7. Its standard atomic mass is
79.904 u, so one mole of Br atoms weighs 79.904 g. The name comes from
Greek: bromos, stench. Bromine is one of only two elements that are liquid at room temperature.
