# Physical properties of Bromine

| property | value |
|---|---|
| density (near room temp) | 3.12 g/cm³ |
| phase at 20 °C | liquid |
| melting point | -7.2 °C |
| boiling point | 58.8 °C |
| appearance | see 09-optical-properties.md — colour #a62929 in this library's palette |

Density is mass divided by volume: a cube of Bromine measuring 1 cm on a side
weighs 3.12 g — the 48-molar-volume and 49-density-compare samples
in this folder draw exactly this mathematics in 3D.

Bromine is a liquid at room temperature — one of the rarest states in the periodic table.
