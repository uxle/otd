## One-paragraph portrait
Iodine is a halogen with atomic number 53: 53 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 53 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,7. Its standard atomic mass is
126.9 u, so one mole of I atoms weighs 126.9 g. The name comes from
Greek: iodes, violet. Iodine vapour is violet — its Greek name means exactly that.
