## One-paragraph portrait
Vanadium is a transition metal with atomic number 23: 23 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 23 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,11,2. Its standard atomic mass is
50.942 u, so one mole of V atoms weighs 50.942 g. The name comes from
Vanadis, Norse goddess of beauty. Vanadium flow batteries can store grid-scale power in giant tanks of liquid.
