## One-paragraph portrait
Gallium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 31: 31 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 31 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,3. Its standard atomic mass is
69.723 u, so one mole of Ga atoms weighs 69.723 g. The name comes from
Latin: Gallia, France. Gallium melts in your hand at 29.8 °C — a spoon of it vanishes in your tea.
