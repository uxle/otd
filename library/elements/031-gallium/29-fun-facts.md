# Fun facts about Gallium

Gallium melts in your hand at 29.8 °C — a spoon of it vanishes in your tea.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 31 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Ga is entry #31
- one mole (69.723 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 5.91 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 29.8 °C is reached in a match flame
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
