## One-paragraph portrait
Lithium is a alkali metal with atomic number 3: 3 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 3 electrons in shells arranged as 2,1. Its standard atomic mass is
6.94 u, so one mole of Li atoms weighs 6.94 g. The name comes from
Greek: lithos, stone. Lithium is the lightest metal — it floats on oil, not just water.
