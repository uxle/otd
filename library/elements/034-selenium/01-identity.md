## One-paragraph portrait
Selenium is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 34: 34 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 34 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,6. Its standard atomic mass is
78.971 u, so one mole of Se atoms weighs 78.971 g. The name comes from
Greek: selene, moon. Selenium conducts electricity better in light — the secret behind early photocopiers.
