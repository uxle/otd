## One-paragraph portrait
Osmium is a transition metal with atomic number 76: 76 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 76 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,14,2. Its standard atomic mass is
190.23 u, so one mole of Os atoms weighs 190.23 g. The name comes from
Greek: osme, smell. Osmium is the densest natural element — a litre of it weighs 22.6 kg.
