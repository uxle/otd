## One-paragraph portrait
Roentgenium is a transition metal with atomic number 111: 111 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 111 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,17,1. Its standard atomic mass is
282.0 u, so one mole of Rg atoms weighs 282.0 g. The name comes from
Wilhelm Roentgen, X-ray discoverer. Roentgenium is predicted to be a noble metal — perhaps gold-like, but it decays in seconds.
