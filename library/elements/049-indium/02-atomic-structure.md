# Atomic structure of Indium

Indium carries 49 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 49
electrons, arranged in 5 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 115, so its nucleus holds
66 neutrons alongside the 49 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 18 electrons
- shell n=5: 3 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 49 (this is what makes it Indium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 66 (mass number 115)
- total nucleons: about 115

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of In's chemistry.
Indium's chemical personality is that of a post-transition metal: form protective oxide skins — aluminium's layer is 4 nanometres of armour.
