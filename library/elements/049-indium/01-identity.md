## One-paragraph portrait
Indium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 49: 49 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 49 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,3. Its standard atomic mass is
114.82 u, so one mole of In atoms weighs 114.82 g. The name comes from
Latin: indicum, indigo (its spectrum line). Every smartphone touchscreen is coated with invisible indium tin oxide.
