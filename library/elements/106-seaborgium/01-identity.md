## One-paragraph portrait
Seaborgium is a transition metal with atomic number 106: 106 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 106 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,12,2. Its standard atomic mass is
269.0 u, so one mole of Sg atoms weighs 269.0 g. The name comes from
Glenn Seaborg, alive at naming. Seaborgium is the first element named after a living person.
