## One-paragraph portrait
Niobium is a transition metal with atomic number 41: 41 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 41 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,12,1. Its standard atomic mass is
92.906 u, so one mole of Nb atoms weighs 92.906 g. The name comes from
Niobe, Greek myth. The LHC accelerates protons using 1,200 tonnes of niobium-titanium superconductor.
