# Characteristic compounds of Hydrogen

Every element builds a family of compounds around its valence electrons
(configuration 1s1). For Hydrogen (H, reactive nonmetal) the
characteristic partners are:

- **oxide** — the compound with oxygen; carbon is stable; phosphorus smoulders in air (white phosphorus!)
- **halide** — with chlorine/fluorine; halides are the classic ionic (or covalent) salts
- **clathrates** — gases form cage compounds with water ice under pressure

Periodic law: similar valence ⇒ similar compounds. That is why the columns of
the periodic table are called families, and why you can predict the oxide
formula of H from its group neighbours before you ever run the reaction.

Fun synthesis fact: Rocket fuel, ammonia synthesis, fuel cells.
