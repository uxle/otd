# Atomic structure of Hydrogen

Hydrogen carries 1 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 1
electrons, arranged in 1 shell around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 1, so its nucleus holds
0 neutrons alongside the 1 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 1 electron

## The nucleus
- protons: 1 (this is what makes it Hydrogen and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 0 (mass number 1)
- total nucleons: about 1

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of H's chemistry.
Hydrogen's chemical personality is that of a reactive nonmetal: carbon is stable; phosphorus smoulders in air (white phosphorus!).
