## One-paragraph portrait
Hydrogen is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 1: 1 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 1 electrons in shells arranged as 1. Its standard atomic mass is
1.008 u, so one mole of H atoms weighs 1.008 g. The name comes from
Greek: hydro + genes, water-forming. The most abundant element in the universe — about 75% of all normal matter.
