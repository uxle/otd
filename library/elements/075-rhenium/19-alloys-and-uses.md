# Alloys and everyday uses of Rhenium

Primary uses: Jet-engine superalloys, catalysts.

An alloy is a metal dissolved in a metal (or a metal with metalloids), tuned for
strength, corrosion resistance, weight or cost. Famous examples: bronze
(copper + tin) launched an age; steel (iron + carbon) built the modern world;
brass (copper + zinc) sings in instruments; solder (tin + lead, now tin + silver)
joins electronics; superalloys (nickel + chromium + rhenium) hold jet
engines together above 1,000 °C.

Rhenium's role in alloys and products: Jet-engine superalloys, catalysts.
