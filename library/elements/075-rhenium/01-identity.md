## One-paragraph portrait
Rhenium is a transition metal with atomic number 75: 75 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 75 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,13,2. Its standard atomic mass is
186.21 u, so one mole of Re atoms weighs 186.21 g. The name comes from
Rhine river (Latin: Rhenus). Rhenium is one of the rarest metals — a single jet engine holds kilograms of it.
