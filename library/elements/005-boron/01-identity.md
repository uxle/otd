## One-paragraph portrait
Boron is a metalloid with atomic number 5: 5 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 5 electrons in shells arranged as 2,3. Its standard atomic mass is
10.81 u, so one mole of B atoms weighs 10.81 g. The name comes from
Arabic: buraq, borax. Boron carbide is one of the hardest materials known — used in tank armour.
