## One-paragraph portrait
Ytterbium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 70: 70 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 70 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
173.05 u, so one mole of Yb atoms weighs 173.05 g. The name comes from
Ytterby, Sweden. Ytterbium clocks are so precise they sense time dilation when lifted 30 cm.
