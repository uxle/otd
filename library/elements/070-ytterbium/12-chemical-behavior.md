# Chemical behaviour of Ytterbium

Ytterbium reacts like a textbook lanthanide (rare earth):

- with air: tarnish quickly in air
- with water: steady — lanthanides tarnish and fizz slowly in water
- with acids: dissolve readily with hydrogen evolution

Electronegativity: 1.1 on the Pauling scale — a measure of how strongly the atom pulls shared electrons.

The octet rule drives everything: atoms react to reach a full outer shell.
Metals lose electrons to become positive cations; nonmetals gain electrons to become anions; the exchange is a salt.
Ytterbium follows these rules so reliably that Mendeleev used family patterns
like this to predict elements before anyone had seen them.
