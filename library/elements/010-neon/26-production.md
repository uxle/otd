# How Neon is produced

Route: **fractional distillation of liquefied air**

The general pipeline for any metal: mine the ore → concentrate it → convert to a
clean intermediate (usually an oxide or chloride) → reduce to metal → refine to
purity. The reduction step is the energy bill: electrolysis for reactive metals
(why aluminium smelters sit next to power plants), carbon reduction for iron,
chemical reduction for the rest.

Nonmetals come from air (nitrogen, oxygen, noble gases — fractional distillation
of liquid air), from mines (sulfur, carbon), or from brines (chlorine by
electrolysis). Superheavy elements (Z ≥ 95) are made one atom at a time in
particle accelerators — production measured in atoms, not tonnes.
