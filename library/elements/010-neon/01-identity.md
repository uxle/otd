## One-paragraph portrait
Neon is a noble gas with atomic number 10: 10 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 10 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8. Its standard atomic mass is
20.18 u, so one mole of Ne atoms weighs 20.18 g. The name comes from
Greek: neos, new. Neon glows the classic red-orange; every other noble-gas colour is an impostor mix.
