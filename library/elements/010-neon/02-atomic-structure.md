# Atomic structure of Neon

Neon carries 10 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 10
electrons, arranged in 2 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 20, so its nucleus holds
10 neutrons alongside the 10 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 10 (this is what makes it Neon and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 10 (mass number 20)
- total nucleons: about 20

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Ne's chemistry.
Neon's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
