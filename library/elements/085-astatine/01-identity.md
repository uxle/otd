## One-paragraph portrait
Astatine is a halogen with atomic number 85: 85 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 85 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,7. Its standard atomic mass is
210.0 u, so one mole of At atoms weighs 210.0 g. The name comes from
Greek: astatos, unstable. Less than 30 grams of astatine exist in Earth's entire crust at any moment.
