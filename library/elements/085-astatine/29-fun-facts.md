# Fun facts about Astatine

Less than 30 grams of astatine exist in Earth's entire crust at any moment.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 85 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, At is entry #85
- one mole (210.0 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 7 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 302 °C is reached in a match flame
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
