# Characteristic compounds of Astatine

Every element builds a family of compounds around its valence electrons
(configuration [Xe] 4f14 5d10 6s2 6p5). For Astatine (At, halogen) the
characteristic partners are:

- **oxide** — the compound with oxygen; no direct burning; fluorine makes even glass smoke
- **halide** — with chlorine/fluorine; halides are the classic ionic (or covalent) salts
- **hydride** — with hydrogen; acidity and reducing power follow periodic trends

Periodic law: similar valence ⇒ similar compounds. That is why the columns of
the periodic table are called families, and why you can predict the oxide
formula of At from its group neighbours before you ever run the reaction.

Fun synthesis fact: Research target for cancer alpha therapy.
