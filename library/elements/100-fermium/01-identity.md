## One-paragraph portrait
Fermium is a actinide with atomic number 100: 100 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 100 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,30,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
257.0 u, so one mole of Fm atoms weighs 257.0 g. The name comes from
Enrico Fermi. Fermium was also discovered in H-bomb debris and kept secret for three years.
