## One-paragraph portrait
Iron is a transition metal with atomic number 26: 26 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 26 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,14,2. Its standard atomic mass is
55.845 u, so one mole of Fe atoms weighs 55.845 g. The name comes from
Latin: ferrum. Iron is the last element a star makes before it dies — fusion stops at iron.
