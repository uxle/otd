# Industrial applications of Neodymium

Neodymium in industry — The strongest permanent magnets, headphones, wind turbines.

Extraction and refining: ion-exchange and solvent extraction from monazite/bastnasite ores, then metallothermic reduction.

Economic notes for a lanthanide (rare earth): supply chains matter. Some elements come
from dozens of countries; others (rare earths, platinum-group metals, tantalum)
come from a handful of mines, which makes them strategically important. Recycling
matters wherever mining is energy-intensive or geopolitically sensitive.

Engineering rule of thumb: pick the cheapest material that survives the
environment for the design life — and every property you rely on (density
7.01 g/cm³, melting point 1024 °C) is on the datasheet.
