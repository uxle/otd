## One-paragraph portrait
Neodymium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 60: 60 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 60 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,22,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
144.24 u, so one mole of Nd atoms weighs 144.24 g. The name comes from
Greek: neos didymos, new twin. A neodymium magnet the size of a coin can lift 10 kg — and shatters if it snaps shut.
