# Magnetic properties of Nickel

Classification: **ferromagnetic — coins and magnets**

Magnetism comes from unpaired electron spins. Iron, cobalt and nickel have whole
regions (domains) of aligned spins — ferromagnetism. Most other metals align
only while a field is applied (paramagnetism). Materials with all spins paired
are pushed out of fields (diamagnetism) — that is how a frog was famously
levitated with a 16-tesla magnet.

Curie point note: above a critical temperature a ferromagnet loses its alignment — that is why a magnet stops working if you heat it red-hot.
Every electron-shells sample (file 40) in this folder shows the spin bookkeeping
that produces this behaviour: count the unpaired electrons in the outer ring.
