## One-paragraph portrait
Nickel is a transition metal with atomic number 28: 28 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 28 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,16,2. Its standard atomic mass is
58.693 u, so one mole of Ni atoms weighs 58.693 g. The name comes from
Swedish: kopparnickel. Earth's core is mostly iron and nickel — a 2,400 km ball of metal.
