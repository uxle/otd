## One-paragraph portrait
Meitnerium is a transition metal with atomic number 109: 109 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 109 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,15,2. Its standard atomic mass is
278.0 u, so one mole of Mt atoms weighs 278.0 g. The name comes from
Lise Meitner. Meitnerium honours Lise Meitner, co-discoverer of nuclear fission, denied her Nobel.
