# Abundance and sources of Meitnerium

Density 37.4 g/cm³ · phase at 20 °C:
solid ·
category transition metal.

Cosmic context: hydrogen and helium dominate the universe (Big Bang ash); stars
forge the rest up to iron; supernovae and neutron-star collisions make the heavy
elements. Earth's crust is mostly oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron and calcium;
the oceans are hydrogen and oxygen with dissolved salts; the atmosphere is
nitrogen and oxygen.

For Meitnerium, terrestrial availability is a defining constraint — superheavy elements exist only atom-by-atom, made in accelerators.
Primary production route: oxide reduction in a blast furnace or via electrolysis of purified solutions.
