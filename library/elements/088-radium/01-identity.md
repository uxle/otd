## One-paragraph portrait
Radium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 88: 88 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 88 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
226.0 u, so one mole of Ra atoms weighs 226.0 g. The name comes from
Latin: radius, ray. 1920s watch dials glowed with radium — the painters licked their brushes and died young.
