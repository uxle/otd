## One-paragraph portrait
Fluorine is a halogen with atomic number 9: 9 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 9 electrons in shells arranged as 2,7. Its standard atomic mass is
18.998 u, so one mole of F atoms weighs 18.998 g. The name comes from
Latin: fluere, to flow. Fluorine attacks almost every material — even glass and some noble gases.
