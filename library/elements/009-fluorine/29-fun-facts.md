# Fun facts about Fluorine

Fluorine attacks almost every material — even glass and some noble gases.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 9 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, F is entry #9
- one mole (18.998 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 0.0017 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- it never sits still long enough to melt visibly — it is a gas at any normal temperature
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
