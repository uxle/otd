## One-paragraph portrait
Livermorium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 116: 116 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 116 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,6. Its standard atomic mass is
293.0 u, so one mole of Lv atoms weighs 293.0 g. The name comes from
Livermore, California lab. Livermorium honours the lab whose town also gave its name to element 115's discoverers.
