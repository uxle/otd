## One-paragraph portrait
Moscovium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 115: 115 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 115 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,5. Its standard atomic mass is
290.0 u, so one mole of Mc atoms weighs 290.0 g. The name comes from
Moscow Oblast. Moscovium decayed away before its discoverers could even weigh a visible speck.
