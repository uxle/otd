## One-paragraph portrait
Nobelium is a actinide with atomic number 102: 102 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 102 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
259.0 u, so one mole of No atoms weighs 259.0 g. The name comes from
Alfred Nobel. Claims to have made nobelium were disputed for years — the Swedes, then Dubna, then Berkeley.
