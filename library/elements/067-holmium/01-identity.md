## One-paragraph portrait
Holmium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 67: 67 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 67 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,29,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
164.93 u, so one mole of Ho atoms weighs 164.93 g. The name comes from
Stockholm (Latin: Holmia). Holmium has the strongest magnetic moment of any element — magnetic focusing uses it.
