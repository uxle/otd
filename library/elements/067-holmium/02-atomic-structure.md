# Atomic structure of Holmium

Holmium carries 67 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 67
electrons, arranged in 6 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 165, so its nucleus holds
98 neutrons alongside the 67 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 29 electrons
- shell n=5: 8 electrons
- shell n=6: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 67 (this is what makes it Holmium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 98 (mass number 165)
- total nucleons: about 165

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Ho's chemistry.
Holmium's chemical personality is that of a lanthanide (rare earth): tarnish quickly in air.
