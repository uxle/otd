## One-paragraph portrait
Erbium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 68: 68 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 68 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,30,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
167.26 u, so one mole of Er atoms weighs 167.26 g. The name comes from
Ytterby, Sweden. The internet runs through erbium: it amplifies light inside every long fibre-optic cable.
