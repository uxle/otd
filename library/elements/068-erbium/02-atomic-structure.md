# Atomic structure of Erbium

Erbium carries 68 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 68
electrons, arranged in 6 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 167, so its nucleus holds
99 neutrons alongside the 68 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 30 electrons
- shell n=5: 8 electrons
- shell n=6: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 68 (this is what makes it Erbium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 99 (mass number 167)
- total nucleons: about 167

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Er's chemistry.
Erbium's chemical personality is that of a lanthanide (rare earth): tarnish quickly in air.
