## One-paragraph portrait
Technetium is a transition metal with atomic number 43: 43 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 43 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,13,2. Its standard atomic mass is
98.0 u, so one mole of Tc atoms weighs 98.0 g. The name comes from
Greek: technetos, artificial. Technetium was the first element made artificially — every atom of it is radioactive.
