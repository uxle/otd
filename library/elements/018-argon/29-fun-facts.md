# Fun facts about Argon

Argon is 1% of the air and is used to protect historical documents from decay.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 18 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Ar is entry #18
- one mole (39.948 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 0.0018 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- it never sits still long enough to melt visibly — it is a gas at any normal temperature
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
