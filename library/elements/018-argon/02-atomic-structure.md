# Atomic structure of Argon

Argon carries 18 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 18
electrons, arranged in 3 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 40, so its nucleus holds
22 neutrons alongside the 18 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 8 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 18 (this is what makes it Argon and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 22 (mass number 40)
- total nucleons: about 40

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Ar's chemistry.
Argon's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
