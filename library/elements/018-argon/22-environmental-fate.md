# Environmental fate of Argon

Where Argon goes after human use depends on solubility, reactivity and
biology. none — these atoms refuse to react with water (or almost anything) — chemistry that decides whether it washes into
rivers, binds to soil, or escapes to air.

Cycles worth knowing: the carbon cycle (atmosphere ↔ ocean ↔ life), the nitrogen
cycle (fixed by bacteria, burned by Haber-Bosch), the water cycle, and the slow
geochemical cycles of metals through rock, water and biota. Heavy metals
accumulate; they do not decay (unless radioactive) and biomagnify up food chains.


Design lesson: a material's end-of-life behaviour (recyclable, reactive, toxic)
is part of its engineering cost.
