## One-paragraph portrait
Argon is a noble gas with atomic number 18: 18 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 18 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,8. Its standard atomic mass is
39.948 u, so one mole of Ar atoms weighs 39.948 g. The name comes from
Greek: argos, lazy. Argon is 1% of the air and is used to protect historical documents from decay.
