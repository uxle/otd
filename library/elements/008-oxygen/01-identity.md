## One-paragraph portrait
Oxygen is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 8: 8 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 8 electrons in shells arranged as 2,6. Its standard atomic mass is
15.999 u, so one mole of O atoms weighs 15.999 g. The name comes from
Greek: oxy + genes, acid-forming. Liquid oxygen is pale blue and magnetic — it sticks to a magnet.
