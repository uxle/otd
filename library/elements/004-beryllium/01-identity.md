## One-paragraph portrait
Beryllium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 4: 4 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 4 electrons in shells arranged as 2,2. Its standard atomic mass is
9.0122 u, so one mole of Be atoms weighs 9.0122 g. The name comes from
Greek: beryllos, the mineral beryl. Beryllium is transparent to X-rays — it was used to make the JWST mirrors.
