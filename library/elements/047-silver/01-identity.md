## One-paragraph portrait
Silver is a transition metal with atomic number 47: 47 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 47 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,1. Its standard atomic mass is
107.87 u, so one mole of Ag atoms weighs 107.87 g. The name comes from
Anglo-Saxon: seolfor. Silver is the best electrical conductor of all elements — better than copper or gold.
