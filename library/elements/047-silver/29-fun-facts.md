# Fun facts about Silver

Silver is the best electrical conductor of all elements — better than copper or gold.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 47 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Ag is entry #47
- one mole (107.87 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 10.49 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 961.8 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
