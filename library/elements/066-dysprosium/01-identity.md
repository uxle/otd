## One-paragraph portrait
Dysprosium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 66: 66 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 66 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,28,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
162.5 u, so one mole of Dy atoms weighs 162.5 g. The name comes from
Greek: dysprositos, hard to get. Without dysprosium, electric-car motors would lose their magnets when they warm up.
