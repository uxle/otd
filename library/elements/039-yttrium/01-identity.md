## One-paragraph portrait
Yttrium is a transition metal with atomic number 39: 39 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 39 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
88.906 u, so one mole of Y atoms weighs 88.906 g. The name comes from
Ytterby, a Swedish village. Four elements come from one tiny Swedish village: yttrium, ytterbium, terbium, erbium.
