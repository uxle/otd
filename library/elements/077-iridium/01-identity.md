## One-paragraph portrait
Iridium is a transition metal with atomic number 77: 77 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 77 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,15,2. Its standard atomic mass is
192.22 u, so one mole of Ir atoms weighs 192.22 g. The name comes from
Iris, Greek goddess of rainbows. The dinosaur-killing asteroid left a thin iridium layer in rocks worldwide — the K-Pg line.
