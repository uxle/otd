## One-paragraph portrait
Oganesson is a noble gas with atomic number 118: 118 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 118 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,8. Its standard atomic mass is
294.0 u, so one mole of Og atoms weighs 294.0 g. The name comes from
Yuri Oganessian (living honouree). Oganesson may be a reactive solid, not a noble gas — the periodic table's strangest ending.
