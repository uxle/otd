# Atomic structure of Oganesson

Oganesson carries 118 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 118
electrons, arranged in 7 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 294, so its nucleus holds
176 neutrons alongside the 118 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 32 electrons
- shell n=6: 18 electrons
- shell n=7: 8 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 118 (this is what makes it Oganesson and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 176 (mass number 294)
- total nucleons: about 294

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Og's chemistry.
Oganesson's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
