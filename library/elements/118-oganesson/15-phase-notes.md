# Phase notes for Oganesson

| phase | exists between |
|---|---|
| solid | below 80 °C |
| liquid | 80 – 350 °C |
| gas | above 350 °C |

A phase diagram maps temperature against pressure. The line between solid and
liquid is melting; between liquid and gas, boiling; above the critical point
(Oganesson there is supercritical fluid where liquid and gas merge). At the
triple point all three coexist — the temperature/pressure where chemists
calibrate thermometers.

Room temperature (20 °C) falls in the solid field of the diagram.
