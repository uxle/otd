## What the numbers mean
Heat is vibrating atoms. In Oganesson the atoms are bound by metallic, covalent,
ionic or van der Waals forces depending on its noble gas nature; the melting
point tells you how strong those bonds are. With a melting point of only 80 °C, Oganesson is among the easiest elements to melt.

Between 80 and 350 °C the element is liquid; above 350 °C it is a gas.
Room temperature (20 °C) sits below the
melting point, which is why Oganesson is a solid in your hand.

Typical thermal conductivity for a noble gas: metals conduct heat by free
electrons (fast), nonmetals by lattice vibrations (slow). Values marked "typical"
