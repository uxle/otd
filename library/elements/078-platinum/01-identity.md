## One-paragraph portrait
Platinum is a transition metal with atomic number 78: 78 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 78 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,16,1. Its standard atomic mass is
195.08 u, so one mole of Pt atoms weighs 195.08 g. The name comes from
Spanish: platina, little silver. All the platinum ever mined would fit in a 7-metre cube — scarcer than gold.
