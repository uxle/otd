## One-paragraph portrait
Scandium is a transition metal with atomic number 21: 21 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 21 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
44.956 u, so one mole of Sc atoms weighs 44.956 g. The name comes from
Latin: Scandia, Scandinavia. Scandium is rare and expensive — one kilogram costs more than a used car.
