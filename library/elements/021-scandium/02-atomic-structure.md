# Atomic structure of Scandium

Scandium carries 21 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 21
electrons, arranged in 4 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 45, so its nucleus holds
24 neutrons alongside the 21 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 9 electrons
- shell n=4: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 21 (this is what makes it Scandium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 24 (mass number 45)
- total nucleons: about 45

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Sc's chemistry.
Scandium's chemical personality is that of a transition metal: slow oxidation — rust for iron, patina for copper, nothing visible for gold.
