# Fun facts about Calcium

Your body contains about 1 kg of calcium — mostly in your skeleton.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 20 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Ca is entry #20
- one mole (40.078 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 1.55 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 842 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
