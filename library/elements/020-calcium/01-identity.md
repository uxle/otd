## One-paragraph portrait
Calcium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 20: 20 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 20 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
40.078 u, so one mole of Ca atoms weighs 40.078 g. The name comes from
Latin: calx, lime. Your body contains about 1 kg of calcium — mostly in your skeleton.
