# History of Calcium

Discovered: **1808** — Humphry Davy.

By 1808 chemistry was becoming quantitative: balance sheets of reactions, spectra, and eventually atomic theory made discovery systematic.

The modern periodic table — Dmitri Mendeleev, 1869 — ordered elements by atomic
weight and left gaps, predicting gallium, scandium and germanium decades before
they were found. Henry Moseley (1913) re-sorted the table by atomic number Z,
fixing the weight anomalies and making the table a quantum statement. The last
piece of the jigsaw, element 117 tennessine, was confirmed only in 2010 — the
table you are using is less than 15 years old at its edges.
