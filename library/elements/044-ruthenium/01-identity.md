## One-paragraph portrait
Ruthenium is a transition metal with atomic number 44: 44 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 44 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,15,1. Its standard atomic mass is
101.07 u, so one mole of Ru atoms weighs 101.07 g. The name comes from
Latin: Ruthenia, Russia. Ruthenium hardens pen tips — the ball of a good pen can outlive its ink.
