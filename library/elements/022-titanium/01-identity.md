## One-paragraph portrait
Titanium is a transition metal with atomic number 22: 22 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 22 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,10,2. Its standard atomic mass is
47.867 u, so one mole of Ti atoms weighs 47.867 g. The name comes from
Greek: Titans, giant gods. Titanium has the best strength-to-weight of any metal — and it is fully biocompatible.
