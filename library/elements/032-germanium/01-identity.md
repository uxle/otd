## One-paragraph portrait
Germanium is a metalloid with atomic number 32: 32 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 32 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,4. Its standard atomic mass is
72.63 u, so one mole of Ge atoms weighs 72.63 g. The name comes from
Latin: Germania, Germany. The first transistor, 1947, was germanium — silicon only took over later.
