# Atomic structure of Germanium

Germanium carries 32 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 32
electrons, arranged in 4 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 73, so its nucleus holds
41 neutrons alongside the 32 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 4 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 32 (this is what makes it Germanium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 41 (mass number 73)
- total nucleons: about 73

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Ge's chemistry.
Germanium's chemical personality is that of a metalloid: slow surface oxidation.
