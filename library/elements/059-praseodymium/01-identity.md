## One-paragraph portrait
Praseodymium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 59: 59 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 59 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,21,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
140.91 u, so one mole of Pr atoms weighs 140.91 g. The name comes from
Greek: prasios didymos, green twin. Praseodymium colours welders' goggles — it blocks the yellow glare of molten metal.
