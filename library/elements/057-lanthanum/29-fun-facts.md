# Fun facts about Lanthanum

Lanthanum glass gives camera lenses their extraordinary clarity.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 57 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, La is entry #57
- one mole (138.91 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 6.162 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 920 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
