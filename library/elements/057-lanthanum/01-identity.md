## One-paragraph portrait
Lanthanum is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 57: 57 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 57 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
138.91 u, so one mole of La atoms weighs 138.91 g. The name comes from
Greek: lanthanein, to escape. Lanthanum glass gives camera lenses their extraordinary clarity.
