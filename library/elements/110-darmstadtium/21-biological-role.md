# Biological role of Darmstadtium

Verdict: no known biological role — radioactive, hence biologically damaging.

Life uses about 25 elements routinely. The big four (H, C, N, O) build the
molecules; minerals (Na, K, Ca, Mg, Cl) run the electrics; trace metals (Fe, Zn,
Cu, Mn, Mo, Co, Se, I) sit in enzyme active sites doing precise chemistry.
Everything else ranges from inert passenger to active poison.


Toxicology principle, attributed to Paracelsus (1538): *"The dose makes the
poison."* Even water and oxygen are lethal in excess; even arsenic's chemistry
has legitimate industrial uses.
