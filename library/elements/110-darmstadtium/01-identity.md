## One-paragraph portrait
Darmstadtium is a transition metal with atomic number 110: 110 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 110 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,16,2. Its standard atomic mass is
281.0 u, so one mole of Ds atoms weighs 281.0 g. The name comes from
Darmstadt, Germany. Darmstadtium was made by firing nickel at lead — one atom every few days.
