## One-paragraph portrait
Thallium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 81: 81 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 81 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,3. Its standard atomic mass is
204.38 u, so one mole of Tl atoms weighs 204.38 g. The name comes from
Greek: thallos, green shoot. Thallium was the poisoner's poison — Agatha Christie built a novel around it.
