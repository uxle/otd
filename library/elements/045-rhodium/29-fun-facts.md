# Fun facts about Rhodium

Rhodium is often pricier than gold — a car's catalytic converter contains grams of it.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 45 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Rh is entry #45
- one mole (102.91 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 12.41 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 1964 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
