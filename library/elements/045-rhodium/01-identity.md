## One-paragraph portrait
Rhodium is a transition metal with atomic number 45: 45 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 45 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,16,1. Its standard atomic mass is
102.91 u, so one mole of Rh atoms weighs 102.91 g. The name comes from
Greek: rhodon, rose. Rhodium is often pricier than gold — a car's catalytic converter contains grams of it.
