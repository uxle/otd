# Fun facts about Cobalt

Cobalt is named after goblins — miners blamed the metal for their ore's tricks.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 27 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Co is entry #27
- one mole (58.933 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 8.9 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 1495 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
