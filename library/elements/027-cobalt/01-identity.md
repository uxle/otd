## One-paragraph portrait
Cobalt is a transition metal with atomic number 27: 27 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 27 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,15,2. Its standard atomic mass is
58.933 u, so one mole of Co atoms weighs 58.933 g. The name comes from
German: kobold, goblin. Cobalt is named after goblins — miners blamed the metal for their ore's tricks.
