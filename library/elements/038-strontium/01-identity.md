## One-paragraph portrait
Strontium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 38: 38 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 38 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
87.62 u, so one mole of Sr atoms weighs 87.62 g. The name comes from
Strontian, a Scottish village. Strontium gives fireworks their brilliant crimson red.
