## One-paragraph portrait
Berkelium is a actinide with atomic number 97: 97 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 97 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,27,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
247.0 u, so one mole of Bk atoms weighs 247.0 g. The name comes from
Berkeley, California. Berkelium was made in a town that named a generation of elements: Berkeley Lab.
