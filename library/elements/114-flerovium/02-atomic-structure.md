# Atomic structure of Flerovium

Flerovium carries 114 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 114
electrons, arranged in 7 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 289, so its nucleus holds
175 neutrons alongside the 114 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 32 electrons
- shell n=6: 18 electrons
- shell n=7: 4 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 114 (this is what makes it Flerovium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 175 (mass number 289)
- total nucleons: about 289

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Fl's chemistry.
Flerovium's chemical personality is that of a post-transition metal: form protective oxide skins — aluminium's layer is 4 nanometres of armour.
