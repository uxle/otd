## One-paragraph portrait
Flerovium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 114: 114 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 114 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,4. Its standard atomic mass is
289.0 u, so one mole of Fl atoms weighs 289.0 g. The name comes from
Georgy Flerov. Flerovium sits at the 'island of stability', where superheavy atoms live a bit longer.
