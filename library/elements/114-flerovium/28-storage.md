# Storage and handling of Flerovium

- form matters: sealed solid container
- environment: standard dry storage
- decay containment: shielded glove box or hot cell; alpha emitters need only skin distance, gamma emitters need lead
- compatibility: keep dissolve unless the oxide skin protects them (aluminium resists until the skin breaks) in mind around acids; slow — aluminium protects itself with an oxide skin, lead grows a passive layer near water

Sample 45 in this folder (the vial) shows the classic storage look: a glass tube
with the element inside, labelled with its symbol. Museums store reactive
elements exactly like this — argon-sealed ampoules on wooden shelves.
