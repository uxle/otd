## One-paragraph portrait
Silicon is a metalloid with atomic number 14: 14 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 14 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,4. Its standard atomic mass is
28.085 u, so one mole of Si atoms weighs 28.085 g. The name comes from
Latin: silex, flint. A silicon wafer is 99.9999999% pure silicon — nine nines, one stray atom per billion.
