## One-paragraph portrait
Krypton is a noble gas with atomic number 36: 36 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 36 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,8. Its standard atomic mass is
83.798 u, so one mole of Kr atoms weighs 83.798 g. The name comes from
Greek: kryptos, hidden. For 23 years, the metre itself was defined by the orange light of krypton-86.
