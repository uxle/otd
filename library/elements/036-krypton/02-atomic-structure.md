# Atomic structure of Krypton

Krypton carries 36 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 36
electrons, arranged in 4 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 84, so its nucleus holds
48 neutrons alongside the 36 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 8 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 36 (this is what makes it Krypton and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 48 (mass number 84)
- total nucleons: about 84

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Kr's chemistry.
Krypton's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
