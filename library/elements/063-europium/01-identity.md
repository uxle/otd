## One-paragraph portrait
Europium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 63: 63 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 63 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,25,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
151.96 u, so one mole of Eu atoms weighs 151.96 g. The name comes from
Europe. Euro notes glow red under UV because of europium — an anti-counterfeit secret.
