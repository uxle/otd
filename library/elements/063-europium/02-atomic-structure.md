# Atomic structure of Europium

Europium carries 63 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 63
electrons, arranged in 6 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 152, so its nucleus holds
89 neutrons alongside the 63 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 25 electrons
- shell n=5: 8 electrons
- shell n=6: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 63 (this is what makes it Europium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 89 (mass number 152)
- total nucleons: about 152

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Eu's chemistry.
Europium's chemical personality is that of a lanthanide (rare earth): tarnish quickly in air.
