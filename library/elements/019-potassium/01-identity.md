## One-paragraph portrait
Potassium is a alkali metal with atomic number 19: 19 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 19 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,8,1. Its standard atomic mass is
39.098 u, so one mole of K atoms weighs 39.098 g. The name comes from
Latin: kalium. Your nerves fire using potassium ions — bananas are radioactive for this reason.
