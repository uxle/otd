## One-paragraph portrait
Copper is a transition metal with atomic number 29: 29 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 29 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,1. Its standard atomic mass is
63.546 u, so one mole of Cu atoms weighs 63.546 g. The name comes from
Latin: cuprum, from Cyprus. Copper is naturally antimicrobial — door handles made of it kill germs on contact.
