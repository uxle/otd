# Fun facts about Gold

Gold is so malleable that 1 gram stretches into a wire 2 km long or beats into 1 m² of leaf.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 79 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Au is entry #79
- one mole (196.97 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 19.32 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 1064.2 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
