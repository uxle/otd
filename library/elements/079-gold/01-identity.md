## One-paragraph portrait
Gold is a transition metal with atomic number 79: 79 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 79 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,1. Its standard atomic mass is
196.97 u, so one mole of Au atoms weighs 196.97 g. The name comes from
Latin: aurum, shining dawn. Gold is so malleable that 1 gram stretches into a wire 2 km long or beats into 1 m² of leaf.
