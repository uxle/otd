## One-paragraph portrait
Mendelevium is a actinide with atomic number 101: 101 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 101 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,31,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
258.0 u, so one mole of Md atoms weighs 258.0 g. The name comes from
Dmitri Mendeleev. Mendelevium was made one atom at a time — just 17 atoms at first.
