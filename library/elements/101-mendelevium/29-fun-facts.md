# Fun facts about Mendelevium

Mendelevium was made one atom at a time — just 17 atoms at first.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 101 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Md is entry #101
- one mole (258.0 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 10.3 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 827 °C is reached in a furnace
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
