## One-paragraph portrait
Molybdenum is a transition metal with atomic number 42: 42 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 42 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,13,1. Its standard atomic mass is
95.95 u, so one mole of Mo atoms weighs 95.95 g. The name comes from
Greek: molybdos, lead-like. Molybdenum keeps its strength at 1,500 °C — armour-piercing rounds use it.
