# Fun facts about Uranium

A marble of natural uranium holds as much energy as 80 tonnes of coal.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 92 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, U is entry #92
- one mole (238.03 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 19.1 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 1135 °C is reached in a furnace
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
