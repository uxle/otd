## One-paragraph portrait
Uranium is a actinide with atomic number 92: 92 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 92 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,21,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
238.03 u, so one mole of U atoms weighs 238.03 g. The name comes from
Uranus, discovered 1781. A marble of natural uranium holds as much energy as 80 tonnes of coal.
