## One-paragraph portrait
Samarium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 62: 62 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 62 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,24,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
150.36 u, so one mole of Sm atoms weighs 150.36 g. The name comes from
Samarskite, the mineral. Samarium-cobalt magnets keep working at 300 °C where neodymium ones give up.
