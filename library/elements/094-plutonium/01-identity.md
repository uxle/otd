## One-paragraph portrait
Plutonium is a actinide with atomic number 94: 94 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 94 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,24,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
244.0 u, so one mole of Pu atoms weighs 244.0 g. The name comes from
Pluto, the next planet out. The Voyager probes still phone home on plutonium heat — 20+ billion km away.
