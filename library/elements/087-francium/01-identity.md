## One-paragraph portrait
Francium is a alkali metal with atomic number 87: 87 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 87 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,8,1. Its standard atomic mass is
223.0 u, so one mole of Fr atoms weighs 223.0 g. The name comes from
France. Francium is so rare and short-lived that nobody has ever seen a visible piece of it.
