# Atomic structure of Francium

Francium carries 87 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 87
electrons, arranged in 7 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 223, so its nucleus holds
136 neutrons alongside the 87 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 18 electrons
- shell n=6: 8 electrons
- shell n=7: 1 electron

## The nucleus
- protons: 87 (this is what makes it Francium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 136 (mass number 223)
- total nucleons: about 223

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Fr's chemistry.
Francium's chemical personality is that of a alkali metal: tarnish within seconds — store under oil.
