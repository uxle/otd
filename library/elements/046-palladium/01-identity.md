## One-paragraph portrait
Palladium is a transition metal with atomic number 46: 46 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 46 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18. Its standard atomic mass is
106.42 u, so one mole of Pd atoms weighs 106.42 g. The name comes from
Pallas, the Greek goddess. Palladium can soak up 900 times its own volume of hydrogen gas.
