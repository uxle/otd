# Industrial applications of Radon

Radon in industry — Radon maps, radiotherapy research.

Extraction and refining: fractional distillation of liquefied air.

Economic notes for a noble gas: supply chains matter. Some elements come
from dozens of countries; others (rare earths, platinum-group metals, tantalum)
come from a handful of mines, which makes them strategically important. Recycling
matters wherever mining is energy-intensive or geopolitically sensitive.

Engineering rule of thumb: pick the cheapest material that survives the
environment for the design life — and every property you rely on (density
0.0097 g/cm³, melting point -71 °C) is on the datasheet.
