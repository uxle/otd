## One-paragraph portrait
Radon is a noble gas with atomic number 86: 86 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 86 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,8. Its standard atomic mass is
222.0 u, so one mole of Rn atoms weighs 222.0 g. The name comes from
Radium (it decays from it). Radon seeps from granite into basements — the invisible natural radiation in every home survey.
