## One-paragraph portrait
Promethium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 61: 61 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 61 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,23,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
145.0 u, so one mole of Pm atoms weighs 145.0 g. The name comes from
Prometheus, fire-bringer. Promethium is the only radioactive lanthanide — it glows faintly in the dark.
