## One-paragraph portrait
Thulium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 69: 69 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 69 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,31,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
168.93 u, so one mole of Tm atoms weighs 168.93 g. The name comes from
Thule, mythical far north. Thulium X-ray machines are small enough to carry into the field — no tube needed.
