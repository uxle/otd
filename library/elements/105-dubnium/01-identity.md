## One-paragraph portrait
Dubnium is a transition metal with atomic number 105: 105 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 105 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,11,2. Its standard atomic mass is
268.0 u, so one mole of Db atoms weighs 268.0 g. The name comes from
Dubna, the research town. Dubnium honours the Russian lab that raced Berkeley to the superheavy elements.
