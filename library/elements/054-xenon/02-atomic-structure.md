# Atomic structure of Xenon

Xenon carries 54 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 54
electrons, arranged in 5 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 131, so its nucleus holds
77 neutrons alongside the 54 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 18 electrons
- shell n=5: 8 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 54 (this is what makes it Xenon and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 77 (mass number 131)
- total nucleons: about 131

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Xe's chemistry.
Xenon's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
