## One-paragraph portrait
Xenon is a noble gas with atomic number 54: 54 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 54 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,8. Its standard atomic mass is
131.29 u, so one mole of Xe atoms weighs 131.29 g. The name comes from
Greek: xenos, stranger. Xenon ion thrusters are pushing spacecraft to asteroids right now.
