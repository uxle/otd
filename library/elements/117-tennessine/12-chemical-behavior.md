# Chemical behaviour of Tennessine

Tennessine reacts like a textbook halogen:

- with air: no direct burning; fluorine makes even glass smoke
- with water: dissolves and reacts — chlorine water is a disinfectant
- with acids: no reaction as metals do — they are already oxidisers

Electronegativity: conventionally not quoted for this element.

The octet rule drives everything: atoms react to reach a full outer shell.
Metals lose electrons to become positive cations; nonmetals gain electrons to become anions; the exchange is a salt.
Tennessine follows these rules so reliably that Mendeleev used family patterns
like this to predict elements before anyone had seen them.
