# Fun facts about Tennessine

Tennessine took a beam of berkelium, 250 days of runs, and three US labs to create.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 117 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Ts is entry #117
- one mole (294.0 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 7.2 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 700 °C is reached in a furnace
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
