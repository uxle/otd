## One-paragraph portrait
Tennessine is a halogen with atomic number 117: 117 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 117 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,7. Its standard atomic mass is
294.0 u, so one mole of Ts atoms weighs 294.0 g. The name comes from
Tennessee. Tennessine took a beam of berkelium, 250 days of runs, and three US labs to create.
