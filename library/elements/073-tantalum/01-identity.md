## One-paragraph portrait
Tantalum is a transition metal with atomic number 73: 73 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 73 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,11,2. Its standard atomic mass is
180.95 u, so one mole of Ta atoms weighs 180.95 g. The name comes from
King Tantalus, tormented in myth. Every smartphone holds a pinch of tantalum in its capacitors — tiny, vital, expensive.
