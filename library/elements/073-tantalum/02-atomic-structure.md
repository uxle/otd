# Atomic structure of Tantalum

Tantalum carries 73 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 73
electrons, arranged in 6 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 181, so its nucleus holds
108 neutrons alongside the 73 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 11 electrons
- shell n=6: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 73 (this is what makes it Tantalum and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 108 (mass number 181)
- total nucleons: about 181

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Ta's chemistry.
Tantalum's chemical personality is that of a transition metal: slow oxidation — rust for iron, patina for copper, nothing visible for gold.
