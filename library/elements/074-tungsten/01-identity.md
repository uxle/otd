## One-paragraph portrait
Tungsten is a transition metal with atomic number 74: 74 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 74 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,12,2. Its standard atomic mass is
183.84 u, so one mole of W atoms weighs 183.84 g. The name comes from
Swedish: tung sten, heavy stone. Tungsten has the highest melting point of any metal — 3,422 °C, white-hot and still solid.
