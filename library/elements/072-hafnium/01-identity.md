## One-paragraph portrait
Hafnium is a transition metal with atomic number 72: 72 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 72 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,10,2. Its standard atomic mass is
178.49 u, so one mole of Hf atoms weighs 178.49 g. The name comes from
Hafnia, Latin for Copenhagen. Hafnium eats neutrons — the opposite of zirconium — so reactors use both deliberately.
