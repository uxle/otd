## One-paragraph portrait
Carbon is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 6: 6 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 6 electrons in shells arranged as 2,4. Its standard atomic mass is
12.011 u, so one mole of C atoms weighs 12.011 g. The name comes from
Latin: carbo, charcoal. Carbon makes nearly ten million known compounds — more than all other elements combined.
