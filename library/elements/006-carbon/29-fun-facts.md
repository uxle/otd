# Fun facts about Carbon

Carbon makes nearly ten million known compounds — more than all other elements combined.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 6 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, C is entry #6
- one mole (12.011 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 2.267 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 3550 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
