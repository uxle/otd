# Crystal structure of Phosphorus

Typical packing: **covalent networks (like diamond) or molecular crystals (like sulfur rings)**

The three densest packings for atoms-as-spheres:
- **BCC** (body-centred cubic) — a sphere at each cube corner plus one in the middle
- **FCC** (face-centred cubic) — corner + face-centre spheres; 74% packing efficiency
- **HCP** (hexagonal close-packed) — layered ABAB stacking, also 74%

The 39-crystal-lattice sample in this folder draws a simple cubic lattice of
Phosphorus atoms; compare its 50% packing with the close-packed 74% above and
you can see why real metals prefer FCC/HCP — more atoms per volume, higher
density 1.823 g/cm³, stronger bonds per gram.
