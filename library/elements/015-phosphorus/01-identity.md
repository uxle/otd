## One-paragraph portrait
Phosphorus is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 15: 15 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 15 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,5. Its standard atomic mass is
30.974 u, so one mole of P atoms weighs 30.974 g. The name comes from
Greek: phosphoros, light-bearer. Phosphorus was discovered in boiled urine — the first element with a known discoverer.
