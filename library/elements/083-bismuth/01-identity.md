## One-paragraph portrait
Bismuth is a post-transition metal with atomic number 83: 83 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 83 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,5. Its standard atomic mass is
208.98 u, so one mole of Bi atoms weighs 208.98 g. The name comes from
German: wissmat, white substance. Bismuth grows rainbow staircase crystals in your kitchen — melt, cool, and watch.
