## One-paragraph portrait
Antimony is a metalloid with atomic number 51: 51 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 51 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,5. Its standard atomic mass is
121.76 u, so one mole of Sb atoms weighs 121.76 g. The name comes from
Greek: anti + monos, not alone. Ancient Egyptians ground antimony into kohl eyeliner 5,000 years ago.
