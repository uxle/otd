## One-paragraph portrait
Nihonium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 113: 113 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 113 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,3. Its standard atomic mass is
286.0 u, so one mole of Nh atoms weighs 286.0 g. The name comes from
Nihon, the Japanese name of Japan. Nihonium was the first element discovered in Asia — named for Japan itself.
