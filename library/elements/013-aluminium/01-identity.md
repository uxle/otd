## One-paragraph portrait
Aluminium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 13: 13 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 13 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,3. Its standard atomic mass is
26.982 u, so one mole of Al atoms weighs 26.982 g. The name comes from
Latin: alumen, alum. A jet plane is roughly 80% aluminium by weight.
