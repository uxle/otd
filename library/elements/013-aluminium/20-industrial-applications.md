# Industrial applications of Aluminium

Aluminium in industry — Aircraft, cans, foil, power lines.

Extraction and refining: electrolytic refining of purified ores.

Economic notes for a post-transition metal: supply chains matter. Some elements come
from dozens of countries; others (rare earths, platinum-group metals, tantalum)
come from a handful of mines, which makes them strategically important. Recycling
is a major source — scrap steel and aluminium are backbone industries.

Engineering rule of thumb: pick the cheapest material that survives the
environment for the design life — and every property you rely on (density
2.7 g/cm³, melting point 660.3 °C) is on the datasheet.
