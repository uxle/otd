## One-paragraph portrait
Hassium is a transition metal with atomic number 108: 108 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 108 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,14,2. Its standard atomic mass is
269.0 u, so one mole of Hs atoms weighs 269.0 g. The name comes from
Hesse, Germany (Latin: Hassias). Hassium's state at room temperature is guessed — only a few hundred atoms ever existed.
