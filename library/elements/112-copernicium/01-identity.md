## One-paragraph portrait
Copernicium is a transition metal with atomic number 112: 112 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 112 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,18,2. Its standard atomic mass is
285.0 u, so one mole of Cn atoms weighs 285.0 g. The name comes from
Nicolaus Copernicus. Copernicium may be a volatile metal or even a gas — relativistic physics bend its electrons.
