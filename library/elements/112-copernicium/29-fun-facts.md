# Fun facts about Copernicium

Copernicium may be a volatile metal or even a gas — relativistic physics bend its electrons.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 112 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Cn is entry #112
- one mole (285.0 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 23.7 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 2850 °C is reached in a furnace
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
