## One-paragraph portrait
Neptunium is a actinide with atomic number 93: 93 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 93 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,22,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
237.0 u, so one mole of Np atoms weighs 237.0 g. The name comes from
Neptune, the next planet out. Neptunium was the first transuranium element ever made — element 93, beyond natural uranium.
