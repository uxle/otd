# Etymology of Curium

Name origin: Marie and Pierre Curie.

Element names carry history in a word: places (germanium, polonium, francium,
americium, nihonium), people (einsteinium, curium, oganesson), gods (titanium,
vanadium, thorium, promethium), colours (caesium's sky-blue line, thallium's
green shoot, indium's indigo), and plain old workman words (iron from many
languages, tungsten = "heavy stone" in Swedish).

The symbols are often Latin: Fe from ferrum, Cu from cuprum, Au from aurum,
Ag from argentum, Pb from plumbum, Sn from stannum, K from kalium, Na from
natrium, W from wolfram. When you read an old chemistry text, you are reading
Latin's last living dialect.
