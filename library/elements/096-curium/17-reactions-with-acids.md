# Curium and acids

Verdict: **dissolve in hydrochloric/nitric acid; uranium dissolves into a glowing green solution**

The classic metal + acid reaction: M + 2HCl → MCl₂ + H₂↑. Whether it happens
depends on where the element sits in the reactivity series — anything above
hydrogen displaces it from the acid; anything below (copper, silver, gold,
platinum) resists even concentrated acids, which is why nitric + hydrochloric
(aqua regia) is needed to dissolve gold.

Curium in the reactivity series: low or inert — protected by thermodynamics or an oxide skin.

Safety note: hydrogen gas from acid reactions is explosive when mixed with air.
