## One-paragraph portrait
Tin is a post-transition metal with atomic number 50: 50 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 50 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,4. Its standard atomic mass is
118.71 u, so one mole of Sn atoms weighs 118.71 g. The name comes from
Latin: stannum. When you bend a bar of tin, the crystals make a crackling 'tin cry'.
