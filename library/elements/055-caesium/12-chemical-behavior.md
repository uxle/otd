# Chemical behaviour of Caesium

Caesium reacts like a textbook alkali metal:

- with air: tarnish within seconds — store under oil
- with water: violent — reacts explosively, may ignite the hydrogen it releases
- with acids: explosive — never add to acid

Electronegativity: 0.79 on the Pauling scale — a measure of how strongly the atom pulls shared electrons.

The octet rule drives everything: atoms react to reach a full outer shell.
Metals lose electrons to become positive cations; nonmetals gain electrons to become anions; the exchange is a salt.
Caesium follows these rules so reliably that Mendeleev used family patterns
like this to predict elements before anyone had seen them.
