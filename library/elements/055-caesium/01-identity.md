## One-paragraph portrait
Caesium is a alkali metal with atomic number 55: 55 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 55 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,8,1. Its standard atomic mass is
132.91 u, so one mole of Cs atoms weighs 132.91 g. The name comes from
Latin: caesius, sky blue. The world's time is set by caesium — 9,192,631,770 oscillations per second.
