## One-paragraph portrait
Chlorine is a halogen with atomic number 17: 17 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 17 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,7. Its standard atomic mass is
35.45 u, so one mole of Cl atoms weighs 35.45 g. The name comes from
Greek: chloros, pale green. Chlorine gas is green-yellow and was used as a chemical weapon in WWI.
