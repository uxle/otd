## One-paragraph portrait
Protactinium is a actinide with atomic number 91: 91 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 91 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,20,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
231.04 u, so one mole of Pa atoms weighs 231.04 g. The name comes from
Greek: protos, parent (of actinium). Protactinium is one of the rarest natural elements — UK once refined 125 g for dating studies.
