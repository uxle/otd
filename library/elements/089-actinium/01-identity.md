## One-paragraph portrait
Actinium is a actinide with atomic number 89: 89 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 89 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
227.0 u, so one mole of Ac atoms weighs 227.0 g. The name comes from
Greek: aktis, ray. Actinium glows pale blue in the dark — radioactivity made visible.
