# Fun facts about Sodium

A pea-sized piece of sodium in water explodes loudly enough to crack glass.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 11 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Na is entry #11
- one mole (22.99 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 0.971 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 97.8 °C is reached in a match flame
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
