## One-paragraph portrait
Sodium is a alkali metal with atomic number 11: 11 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 11 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,1. Its standard atomic mass is
22.99 u, so one mole of Na atoms weighs 22.99 g. The name comes from
English: soda. A pea-sized piece of sodium in water explodes loudly enough to crack glass.
