# Abundance and sources of Terbium

Density 8.23 g/cm³ · phase at 20 °C:
solid ·
category lanthanide (rare earth).

Cosmic context: hydrogen and helium dominate the universe (Big Bang ash); stars
forge the rest up to iron; supernovae and neutron-star collisions make the heavy
elements. Earth's crust is mostly oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron and calcium;
the oceans are hydrogen and oxygen with dissolved salts; the atmosphere is
nitrogen and oxygen.

For Terbium, terrestrial availability is a supply-chain question, not an existence question.
Primary production route: ion-exchange and solvent extraction from monazite/bastnasite ores, then metallothermic reduction.
