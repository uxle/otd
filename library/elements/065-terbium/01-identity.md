## One-paragraph portrait
Terbium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 65: 65 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 65 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,27,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
158.93 u, so one mole of Tb atoms weighs 158.93 g. The name comes from
Ytterby, Sweden. Terbium alloy 'Terfenol-D' changes shape in magnetic fields — it hums in sonar.
