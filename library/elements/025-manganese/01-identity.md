## One-paragraph portrait
Manganese is a transition metal with atomic number 25: 25 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 25 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,13,2. Its standard atomic mass is
54.938 u, so one mole of Mn atoms weighs 54.938 g. The name comes from
Latin: magnes, magnet. Deep-sea nodules of manganese grow 1 cm per million years.
