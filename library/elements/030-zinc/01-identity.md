## One-paragraph portrait
Zinc is a transition metal with atomic number 30: 30 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 30 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,2. Its standard atomic mass is
65.38 u, so one mole of Zn atoms weighs 65.38 g. The name comes from
German: zinke, toothy (spiky crystals). Your body uses zinc in over 300 enzymes — taste and immunity need it.
