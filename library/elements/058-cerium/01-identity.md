## One-paragraph portrait
Cerium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 58: 58 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 58 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,19,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
140.12 u, so one mole of Ce atoms weighs 140.12 g. The name comes from
Ceres, the dwarf planet. Cerium sparks are the flash in every lighter — strike, spark, flame.
