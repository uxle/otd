## One-paragraph portrait
Einsteinium is a actinide with atomic number 99: 99 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 99 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,29,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
252.0 u, so one mole of Es atoms weighs 252.0 g. The name comes from
Albert Einstein. Einsteinium was found in the fallout of the first hydrogen-bomb test — born in a fireball.
