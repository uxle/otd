# Atomic structure of Einsteinium

Einsteinium carries 99 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 99
electrons, arranged in 7 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 252, so its nucleus holds
153 neutrons alongside the 99 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 29 electrons
- shell n=6: 8 electrons
- shell n=7: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 99 (this is what makes it Einsteinium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 153 (mass number 252)
- total nucleons: about 252

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Es's chemistry.
Einsteinium's chemical personality is that of a actinide: oxidise; fine uranium chips can catch fire spontaneously.
