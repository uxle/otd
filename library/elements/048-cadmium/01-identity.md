## One-paragraph portrait
Cadmium is a transition metal with atomic number 48: 48 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 48 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,2. Its standard atomic mass is
112.41 u, so one mole of Cd atoms weighs 112.41 g. The name comes from
Greek: kadmia, calamine ore. Van Gogh's brilliant yellows were cadmium pigments — now they are being phased out.
