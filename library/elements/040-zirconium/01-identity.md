## One-paragraph portrait
Zirconium is a transition metal with atomic number 40: 40 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 40 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,10,2. Its standard atomic mass is
91.224 u, so one mole of Zr atoms weighs 91.224 g. The name comes from
Arabic: zargun, gold-coloured. Zirconium barely absorbs neutrons — every nuclear submarine is clad in it.
