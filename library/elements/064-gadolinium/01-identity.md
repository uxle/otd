## One-paragraph portrait
Gadolinium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 64: 64 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 64 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,25,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
157.25 u, so one mole of Gd atoms weighs 157.25 g. The name comes from
Gadolinite, the mineral. Gadolinium is magnetic just below room temperature and warms when magnetised.
