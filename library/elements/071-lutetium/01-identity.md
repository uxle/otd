## One-paragraph portrait
Lutetium is a lanthanide (rare earth) with atomic number 71: 71 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 71 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,9,2. Its standard atomic mass is
174.97 u, so one mole of Lu atoms weighs 174.97 g. The name comes from
Lutetia, the Roman Paris. Lutetium crystals in PET scanners catch the flash of cancer-tracing positrons.
