# Lutetium and water

Verdict: **steady — lanthanides tarnish and fizz slowly in water**

Density test: 9.841 g/cm³ vs water 0.997 g/cm³ ⇒
sinks.
(Archimedes: an object floats when it displaces its own weight of fluid before
it is fully submerged — sample 49 draws this comparison in 3D.)

For the metals that react, the chemistry is: M + H₂O → M(OH) + H₂ (or fuller
oxides), and the hydrogen can ignite. Sodium and potassium are the classic
demonstration — and the reason they live under oil. Noble metals ignore water
entirely; that is why gold treasure survives shipwrecks.
