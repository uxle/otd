## One-paragraph portrait
Tellurium is a metalloid with atomic number 52: 52 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 52 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,6. Its standard atomic mass is
127.6 u, so one mole of Te atoms weighs 127.6 g. The name comes from
Latin: tellus, the Earth. Tellurium is rarer than gold in Earth's crust, yet it costs far less.
