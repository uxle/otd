## One-paragraph portrait
Rutherfordium is a transition metal with atomic number 104: 104 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 104 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,10,2. Its standard atomic mass is
267.0 u, so one mole of Rf atoms weighs 267.0 g. The name comes from
Ernest Rutherford. Rutherfordium was fought over for 30 years before IUPAC settled the name in 1997.
