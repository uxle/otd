# Fun facts about Lead

Roman plumbing gave lead its symbol Pb — from plumbum, the root of the word plumber.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 82 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Pb is entry #82
- one mole (207.2 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 11.34 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 327.5 °C is reached in a match flame
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
