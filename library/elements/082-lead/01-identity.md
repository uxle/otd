## One-paragraph portrait
Lead is a post-transition metal with atomic number 82: 82 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 82 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,4. Its standard atomic mass is
207.2 u, so one mole of Pb atoms weighs 207.2 g. The name comes from
Anglo-Saxon: lead. Roman plumbing gave lead its symbol Pb — from plumbum, the root of the word plumber.
