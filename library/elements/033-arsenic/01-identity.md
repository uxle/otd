## One-paragraph portrait
Arsenic is a metalloid with atomic number 33: 33 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 33 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,5. Its standard atomic mass is
74.922 u, so one mole of As atoms weighs 74.922 g. The name comes from
Greek: arsenikon, yellow pigment. Arsenic was the poison of kings — tasteless, colourless, and undetectable for centuries.
