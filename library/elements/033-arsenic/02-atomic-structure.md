# Atomic structure of Arsenic

Arsenic carries 33 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 33
electrons, arranged in 4 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 75, so its nucleus holds
42 neutrons alongside the 33 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 5 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 33 (this is what makes it Arsenic and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 42 (mass number 75)
- total nucleons: about 75

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of As's chemistry.
Arsenic's chemical personality is that of a metalloid: slow surface oxidation.
