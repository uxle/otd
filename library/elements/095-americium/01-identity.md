## One-paragraph portrait
Americium is a actinide with atomic number 95: 95 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 95 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,25,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
243.0 u, so one mole of Am atoms weighs 243.0 g. The name comes from
The Americas. Your smoke detector may hold a speck of americium — a home-sized radiation watchdog.
