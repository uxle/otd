# Fun facts about Americium

Your smoke detector may hold a speck of americium — a home-sized radiation watchdog.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 95 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Am is entry #95
- one mole (243.0 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 12 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 1176 °C is reached in a furnace
- radioactive: handle with time, distance, shielding
