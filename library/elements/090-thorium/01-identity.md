## One-paragraph portrait
Thorium is a actinide with atomic number 90: 90 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 90 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,10,2. Its standard atomic mass is
232.04 u, so one mole of Th atoms weighs 232.04 g. The name comes from
Thor, the Norse thunder god. Thorium reactors could run on an abundant fuel that cannot easily be weaponised.
