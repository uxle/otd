# Abundance and sources of Thorium

Density 11.72 g/cm³ · phase at 20 °C:
solid ·
category actinide.

Cosmic context: hydrogen and helium dominate the universe (Big Bang ash); stars
forge the rest up to iron; supernovae and neutron-star collisions make the heavy
elements. Earth's crust is mostly oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron and calcium;
the oceans are hydrogen and oxygen with dissolved salts; the atmosphere is
nitrogen and oxygen.

For Thorium, terrestrial availability is a supply-chain question, not an existence question.
Primary production route: uranium ore conversion, enrichment, and reactor breeding (for heavier actinides).
