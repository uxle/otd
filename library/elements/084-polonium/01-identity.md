## One-paragraph portrait
Polonium is a post-transition metal with atomic number 84: 84 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 84 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,6. Its standard atomic mass is
209.0 u, so one mole of Po atoms weighs 209.0 g. The name comes from
Poland, Marie Curie's homeland. Polonium is so radioactive that a gram heats itself to 500 °C and glows blue.
