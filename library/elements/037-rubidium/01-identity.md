## One-paragraph portrait
Rubidium is a alkali metal with atomic number 37: 37 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 37 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,8,1. Its standard atomic mass is
85.468 u, so one mole of Rb atoms weighs 85.468 g. The name comes from
Latin: rubidus, deep red. Rubidium atomic clocks lose one second every 100 million years.
