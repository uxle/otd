# Thermal properties of Rubidium

| quantity | °C | K |
|---|---|---|
| melting point | 39.3 | 312.45 |
| boiling point | 688 | 961.15 |

## What the numbers mean
Heat is vibrating atoms. In Rubidium the atoms are bound by metallic, covalent,
ionic or van der Waals forces depending on its alkali metal nature; the melting
point tells you how strong those bonds are. With a melting point of only 39.3 °C, Rubidium is among the easiest elements to melt.

Between 39.3 and 688 °C the element is liquid; above 688 °C it is a gas.
Room temperature (20 °C) sits below the
melting point, which is why Rubidium is a solid in your hand.

Typical thermal conductivity for a alkali metal: metals conduct heat by free
electrons (fast), nonmetals by lattice vibrations (slow). Values marked "typical"
in this library are category averages, not measured constants.
