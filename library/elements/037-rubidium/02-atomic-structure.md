# Atomic structure of Rubidium

Rubidium carries 37 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 37
electrons, arranged in 5 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 85, so its nucleus holds
48 neutrons alongside the 37 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 8 electrons
- shell n=5: 1 electron

## The nucleus
- protons: 37 (this is what makes it Rubidium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 48 (mass number 85)
- total nucleons: about 85

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Rb's chemistry.
Rubidium's chemical personality is that of a alkali metal: tarnish within seconds — store under oil.
