## One-paragraph portrait
Magnesium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 12: 12 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 12 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
24.305 u, so one mole of Mg atoms weighs 24.305 g. The name comes from
Greek: Magnesia region. Every green leaf runs on a magnesium atom at the heart of chlorophyll.
