# Fun facts about Magnesium

Every green leaf runs on a magnesium atom at the heart of chlorophyll.

More angles on the same element:
- atomic number 12 — in a periodic table sorted by Z, Mg is entry #12
- one mole (24.305 g) holds 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000 atoms
- a 1 cm³ cube weighs 1.738 g — sample 31 is exactly this cube
- its melting point 650 °C is reached in a furnace
- stable: it has outlived the Earth so far and will outlive us
