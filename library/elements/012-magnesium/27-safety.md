# Safety profile of Magnesium

Hazard class: **low toxicity but powders are flammable**

Rules that apply to every element:
1. Know the form — bulk metal, fine powder, compound and solution can have wildly different hazards.
2. Dusts of almost any metal are a fire/explosion risk; vent them, never sweep them.
3. Radioactive materials are controlled by time, distance and shielding.
4. The dose makes the poison — but for some elements no safe dose is known.

Storage: sealed, dry, inert atmosphere for reactive forms.
Dispose of Magnesium waste through proper channels — heavy metals and
radionuclides outlive careless decisions.
