# Physical properties of Mercury

| property | value |
|---|---|
| density (near room temp) | 13.534 g/cm³ |
| phase at 20 °C | liquid |
| melting point | -38.8 °C |
| boiling point | 356.7 °C |
| appearance | see 09-optical-properties.md — colour #b8b8d0 in this library's palette |

Density is mass divided by volume: a cube of Mercury measuring 1 cm on a side
weighs 13.534 g — the 48-molar-volume and 49-density-compare samples
in this folder draw exactly this mathematics in 3D.

Mercury is a liquid at room temperature — one of the rarest states in the periodic table.
