## One-paragraph portrait
Mercury is a transition metal with atomic number 80: 80 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 80 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,18,2. Its standard atomic mass is
200.59 u, so one mole of Hg atoms weighs 200.59 g. The name comes from
Latin: hydrargyrum, liquid silver. Mercury is the only metal liquid at room temperature — and it pours like silver syrup.
