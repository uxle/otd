# Atomic structure of Mercury

Mercury carries 80 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 80
electrons, arranged in 6 shells around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 201, so its nucleus holds
121 neutrons alongside the 80 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons
- shell n=2: 8 electrons
- shell n=3: 18 electrons
- shell n=4: 32 electrons
- shell n=5: 18 electrons
- shell n=6: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 80 (this is what makes it Mercury and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 121 (mass number 201)
- total nucleons: about 201

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of Hg's chemistry.
Mercury's chemical personality is that of a transition metal: slow oxidation — rust for iron, patina for copper, nothing visible for gold.
