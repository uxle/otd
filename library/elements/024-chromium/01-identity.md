## One-paragraph portrait
Chromium is a transition metal with atomic number 24: 24 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 24 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,13,1. Its standard atomic mass is
51.996 u, so one mole of Cr atoms weighs 51.996 g. The name comes from
Greek: chroma, colour. Ruby red and emerald green both come from traces of the same element: chromium.
