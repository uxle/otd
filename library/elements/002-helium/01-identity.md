## One-paragraph portrait
Helium is a noble gas with atomic number 2: 2 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 2 electrons in shells arranged as 2. Its standard atomic mass is
4.0026 u, so one mole of He atoms weighs 4.0026 g. The name comes from
Greek: helios, the Sun. Helium was found in the Sun's spectrum before it was found on Earth.
