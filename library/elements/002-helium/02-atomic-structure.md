# Atomic structure of Helium

Helium carries 2 protons. A neutral atom therefore holds exactly 2
electrons, arranged in 1 shell around the
nucleus. The most common isotope has mass number 4, so its nucleus holds
2 neutrons alongside the 2 protons.

## Electron shells
- shell n=1: 2 electrons

## The nucleus
- protons: 2 (this is what makes it Helium and nothing else)
- neutrons: about 2 (mass number 4)
- total nucleons: about 4

Electrons fill shells from the inside out: 2 in the first shell, 8 in the second,
18 in the third (with sub-shell bookkeeping the pattern continues 8, 18, 32 …).
The outermost electrons — the valence electrons — decide all of He's chemistry.
Helium's chemical personality is that of a noble gas: none.
