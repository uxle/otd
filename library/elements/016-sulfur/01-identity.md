## One-paragraph portrait
Sulfur is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 16: 16 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 16 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,6. Its standard atomic mass is
32.06 u, so one mole of S atoms weighs 32.06 g. The name comes from
Sanskrit: sulvere. Sulfuric acid is among the most produced chemicals in the world — the 'king of chemicals'.
