## One-paragraph portrait
Nitrogen is a reactive nonmetal with atomic number 7: 7 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 7 electrons in shells arranged as 2,5. Its standard atomic mass is
14.007 u, so one mole of N atoms weighs 14.007 g. The name comes from
Greek: nitron genes, niter-forming. About 78% of every breath you take is nitrogen gas.
