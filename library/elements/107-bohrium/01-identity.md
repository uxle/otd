## One-paragraph portrait
Bohrium is a transition metal with atomic number 107: 107 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 107 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,32,32,13,2. Its standard atomic mass is
270.0 u, so one mole of Bh atoms weighs 270.0 g. The name comes from
Niels Bohr. Bohrium's chemistry was tested atom by atom — each experiment handled a handful of atoms.
