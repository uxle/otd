## One-paragraph portrait
Barium is a alkaline earth metal with atomic number 56: 56 protons in the nucleus
balanced by 56 electrons in shells arranged as 2,8,18,18,8,2. Its standard atomic mass is
137.33 u, so one mole of Ba atoms weighs 137.33 g. The name comes from
Greek: barys, heavy. Barium meals make the gut visible on X-rays — inert sulfate only.
