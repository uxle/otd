## The nucleus
The protons all repel each other; the strong nuclear force and the neutrons hold
the nucleus together. For light elements stability favours neutrons ≈ protons;
for heavy elements you need roughly 1.5 neutrons per proton. Barium
(56 protons, ~81 neutrons) sits comfortably inside the
valley of stability — hence its stable isotopes.

## Binding energy
Iron-56 sits at the peak of the binding-energy curve: fusing lighter elements
releases energy up to iron, and splitting heavier ones releases energy too.
That single curve explains stars, reactors and bombs.
