WHAT'S UNDER THE SYNTAX

The physics is real, not decorative

Every scene is measured, not just drawn. Mass comes from the mesh volume and the material density; nothing floats or overlaps unless the numbers say it should.

Nothing overlaps

simulate: settle drops every body under gravity until it rests on the ground or another part, then simulate: solidity audits the result. Contacts are checked to under 0.05 mm of interpenetration.

A real energy ledger

Kinetic and potential energy, each broken into its own forms — mechanical, thermal, radiant, chemical, nuclear, elastic — computed with the textbook law for each: PE = mgh, E = mc², U = ½kx².

Chemistry that reacts

Liquids mix or refuse each other by polarity and settle into density-sorted layers; gases diffuse into one phase; reactive pairs don't mix — they burn, with a balanced, independently verified equation.

Waves, fields, and light

Doppler shift, echo ranging, Snell refraction, Wien's law, magnetic dipole forces and Faraday EMF — each keyed to the material actually in the scene, not a generic constant.

It knows what you're made of

simulate: atom turns every part's measured mass into a particle census — protons, neutrons, electrons, counted for real — and simulate: decay reports the live radioactivity in becquerels. The quarks in a proton weigh 9.1 MeV; the proton weighs 938.3: 99% of mass is gluon field energy.

An AI that abstains

The vendored reasoning engine answers physics questions in plain language — otd --ai "why does a ship float?" — and every answer is verification-gated: VERIFIED or ABSTAINED, never a guess.

It can see its own scene

otd --perceive bench.otd renders a stereo pair and reports what's there — object positions, distances, and relations — with honest uncertainty on every number, not false precision.

THE ENGINE, NOT THE SURFACE

Three tiers. One friendly language.

The syntax stays simple on purpose — under 60 keywords, learnable in five minutes — while the engine underneath does real computational geometry and runs on a bytecode virtual machine.

Lite

What you type

Scenes, shapes, materials, if / for / while, comparisons and logic, ranges, and assert — plain sentences a beginner can read back.

→
Deep

The computational core

Half-edge topology, Discrete Exterior Calculus, signed-distance blending, and an XPBD solver turn those sentences into a closed, measurable mesh.

→
ASM

Real bytecode

Your arithmetic compiles to a 32-byte vector bytecode and runs on a hand-written virtual machine — inspect it directly with --vm-dump.

THE VIDEO STUDIO

Eight cameras. A real H.264 file.

--video out.mp4 --cams 8 renders eight views around the scene and encodes them into an actual Baseline H.264 bitstream, muxed into MP4 by a hand-written ISO-BMFF writer — validated bit-exact against reference decoders. No video library, no GPU encoder, no external dependency.

THE FORGE UPDATE

Self make anything

One plain-language goal in, a complete .otd program out — written, compiled, and repaired by the engine itself until it passes. The neural network library and the deep-learning framework that do the learning were self-made too, in the project's own zero-dependency tradition.

terminal 
otd --make "a titanium ball dropping into water from 1m"

SYNTHESIZED OUTPUT

scene "Titanium ball — dropping in water"

environment: water

titaniumBall = sphere(r: 3cm) at (0mm, 1m, 0)
              material: titanium

simulate: drop
simulate: time
ask "impact speed?"

1 solid part, 506.0 g, zero repair rounds — then it films itself: --cams 8 --video.

A synthesizer with a safety net

--make scans the goal for materials, shapes, sizes and motion, plans the physics, and emits idiomatic OTD — then compiles its own output and repairs it (safe sizes, fallback materials) until the engine accepts it.

nn.rs — a neural net, self-made

A single-file library: tensors with cache-blocked matrix multiplication, ReLU/GELU/CELU activations, softmax cross-entropy with analytic gradients, and Sgd/Adam/AdamW optimizers with bias correction.

OTD-Burn — a framework in miniature

Backend trait, reverse-mode autodiff tape — every gradient checked against finite differences — modules with checkpoints, and a Learner with early stopping. otd --train learns freefall: v = √(2gh).

The scene is the dataset

Statistics and astronomy joined the engine: Lanczos log-gamma, the normal CDF and its inverse, Kepler's equation by Newton's method, Hohmann transfers — the parts of a scene become samples, heights become orbital altitudes.
